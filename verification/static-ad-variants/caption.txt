More completed tasks show that you can execute. They don't always show how you make a difficult call.

For your next recommendation, write three short sentences:
• I would choose this option.
• The cost or risk I accept is this.
• I would reconsider if this changed.

Fill in the specifics. That makes your reasoning easier to examine than a confident answer alone.

In Proving Ground, decision practice is marked against a visible standard. Each score explains what a top mark requires and where your answer fell short. Use that feedback to spot what your next recommendation needs.
