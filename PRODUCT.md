# FirstChair Studio — product

Current scope: 13 October 2026. `CONTENT_PROCESS.md` is the authoritative generation contract. It supersedes historical website-to-content and forced sales-story instructions.

## Customer and purpose
Help stretched solopreneurs turn an article or customer-call transcript into useful, editable organic content without rebuilding it in Canva. Save writing and design effort while preserving source meaning and readable typography. No claims of measured reach, sales or time savings yet.

## Inputs and output
A website supplies colours and fonts only. Exactly one article or call transcript supplies all content. Output is a coherent carousel and a static expressing the same core message, using the frozen approved layout. Website copy, logos, photographs, product offers and audience assumptions are not writing inputs.

## Journey
1. Enter the website.
2. Choose Article or Call transcript; paste text or upload a supported document. A source is required; there is no website-only skip.
3. Connect ChatGPT if needed. Show actual styling, writing and checking progress.
4. Inspect Carousel / Statics on the left and edit image text on the right.
5. Preserve the existing download and editing controls and restrictions; billing integration remains separate. Do not claim downloadable PNGs are layered editable designs.

Keep the existing sky, interface colours, headline and website field. Relay is inspiration for smooth interaction, not a template for our content rules. No team-size, marketing-tool or sales-goal questionnaire is required.

## Quality and reliability
Use the standard process in `CONTENT_PROCESS.md` for every supported topic. A useful guide need not imitate a customer confession; an experience must not gain an invented transformation. Check script accuracy separately from rendered layout. Preserve drafts on failures and describe missing source/style evidence plainly. Do not substitute business text for blocked website styling.

## Samples and scope
Sample preview remains a collection of saved reviewed/edited examples, not proof of unattended output quality. Historical samples remain readable. Existing reel editing is separate and unchanged. No scheduling, analytics, payments or new publishing integrations are added by this change. GitHub and Convex remain the stack.

## Success
Measure waiting time separately from active editing time, source fidelity, story clarity, colour/font fidelity, export readability and whether founders can use the output without rebuilding it. Passing code checks is not proof of creative quality.

## Latest learning: preserve intent before choosing structure
Condense the article or transcript faithfully. Select steps for guides, explanation for explainers, differences for comparisons, reasons for opinions, and a story only for a genuine experience sequence. Otherwise group transcript insights by topic. Preserve caveats, remove repetition, split at natural breaks, and never invent drama or force a sales ending. Follow the examples and acceptance rules in `CONTENT_PROCESS.md`.
