# Founder’s Find visual reference

Observed live homepage, 4 October 2026: https://foundersfind.club/ .

- Headlines: Georgia serif, regular weight. Selected phrases use pink italic serif.
- Wordmark: serif “Founder’s” with pink italic “Find”.
- Body: Inter, quieter grey. White space and fine rules establish structure.
- Colour: white #ffffff, ink #0f0f12, pink #ff307e. Pink is selective emphasis rather than a heavy full-width footer.
- Visuals: thin black isometric diagrams, light connecting lines and a selected pink tile. The hero connects opportunity, people, selection, proof and capability.
- User direction: no AI faces in Founder’s Find creatives. Use type and brand diagrams in static posts, carousels and motion reels. Do not automatically request portrait generation for this brand.

The app implements original geometric diagrams in this language, not invented customer photos, fake testimonials or performance charts. Source-backed copy remains unchanged by this visual correction.
