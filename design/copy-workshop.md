## Maid2Clean — domestic service

Reader: UK householder considering who to invite into their home. Source: verification/quality/after/maid-copy.json, brand.facts. Source says meeting/approval before cleaning and that the first suggested cleaner need not be accepted.

Earlier opening: “Same home. Different cleaning brief.” Critique: clever but the practical concern is buried. Proposed carousel directly answers an important choice with documented process, without claiming risk disappears.

### Carousel

1. **Can you meet your cleaner before the first clean?** With Maid2Clean, you can meet the proposed cleaner and discuss the tasks before approving the choice.
2. **Make the introduction part of the decision.** Maid2Clean arranges a meeting after proposing a cleaner. You can talk through the cleaning you want done.
3. **Discuss the jobs that matter to you.** Use the meeting to explain your priorities. What needs doing—and how thoroughly—helps define the cleaning brief.
4. **You don’t have to accept the first person.** Maid2Clean says there is no need to agree to the first cleaner it sends. The introduction gives you a choice, not a guarantee of a perfect match.
5. **Understand the process before requesting help.** Read the domestic-cleaning FAQs, then enquire about the cleaning you need.

## Lessons applied to the shared generator

- Separate our customer from their buyer; unfamiliar brands need understandable category context.
- Judge the idea and delivered payoff before polishing language.
- Sensory desire, honest demonstrations and useful distinctions can qualify without a checklist.
- Choose complementary carousel/static ideas; do not force every category into pain copy.
- Use real product/process facts for credibility when customer proof is absent.
- Review and rewrite once; retain failed drafts and explain remaining problems.

Human editorial judgement above is not a blind study or measured conversion result. Fresh generator outputs and browser verification belong in the run record, separately from these proposed samples.
