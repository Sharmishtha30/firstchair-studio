# Brand-specific creative direction

This replaces the earlier Founder’s Find-only art direction. The studio interface is separate from every customer's exported content.

Medium: each submitted site's own product/editorial photography where relevant, plus structured typography and original useful diagrams. Never generated photos, faces, logos, fake interfaces or invented data.
Palette: inspect the submitted site's default background, ink and primary accent. Preserve those roles. Missing values are labelled fallbacks. No global Founder’s Find palette.
Typography: the site's observed heading/body character and hierarchy. Use legitimately available font files; otherwise clearly name the substitute. Editorial serif sites retain serif character; bold product brands retain their declared display character, including heavy serif where present. Respect 80px outer margins and body text at least40px; rewrite content that does not fit.
Composition: choose a deliberate story and a visual idea, not fixed slide slots. Editorial essays, expressive posters, source-image compositions, two-column explanations and useful lists may appear wherever the content earns them. No template-count quota, compulsory cover comparison or giant CTA arrow. Learn premise/detail/takeaway hierarchy and image/type interplay from GOLDEN_SET without copying its branding or assuming missing endings. One visual family per carousel, varied pace. A static delivers a different complete point with its own composition. Source product photos preserve proportions and useful details.
Mood: derived from the individual site's offer and voice, never one universal mood imposed on every brand.
Never: generic inspirational slogans, unrelated AI portraits, decorative fake charts, unverified proof, invented testimonials, unrelated partner logos, paragraph screenshots, identical text boxes across all five slides.

Verification: compare source website, 360px previews, every exported PNG and rendered PDF page. A functioning download is not creative-quality evidence. Founder approval remains the final human quality check.
