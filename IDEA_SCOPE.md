# Website to Instagram studio — approved scope, 4 October 2026

The founder enters a website. The studio reads public brand and product information and produces three finished Instagram formats: a carousel, a static post and a reel, including content, imagery, video and an AI-avatar capability. Founders Find (https://foundersfind.club/) is the first full example. Relay Studio is a product reference, not copied branding or a promise of identical capabilities.

Hooks must be short, clear and specific to the audience's pain and emotion. Each piece follows hook, value and CTA. Claims must be supported by the source website; hypothetical scenarios must be labelled as such. Tone and inferred emotions must not be presented as verified brand guidelines or customer testimony.

Use real public ad-library content as attributed inspiration when accessible. Never invent imported ads or performance metrics. Provide source links and report access failures.

Use Convex for database, backend, background work and storage; GitHub for code. Preserve the existing ChatGPT-plan connection, never silently fall back to a paid API. Prepare Seedance 2.5 integration for genuine generated video and clearly report missing provider access. Produce downloadable Instagram assets and captions, test browser interaction and file output, and commit working steps. No deployment or social posting, CRM, extra channels or scheduling. Work autonomously without product questions while the founder rests.

This explicitly replaces the original transcript-only/no-video scope. The original transcript writer remains available as a secondary tool while the new website flow becomes the primary experience.
