# Content generation instructions

The single authoritative instruction set is `CONTENT_PROCESS.md`; executable rules and schemas are in `convex/contentPolicy.ts`.

Website: colours and fonts only. Article/transcript: all content. Keep these inputs separate in code, not just in a prompt. Choose the structure from the source, write a connected script, split it into slides, review meaning, then render the frozen format. Do not reintroduce website marketing angles, forced buying ideas, mandatory brand answers, separate platform story generation or variable product-image layouts.

Use source evidence checks and varied article/transcript trials to evaluate improvements. Saved gallery examples are historical references, never factual material for a new post.

## Latest learning: preserve intent before choosing structure
Condense the article or transcript faithfully. Select steps for guides, explanation for explainers, differences for comparisons, reasons for opinions, and a story only for a genuine experience sequence. Otherwise group transcript insights by topic. Preserve caveats, remove repetition, split at natural breaks, and never invent drama or force a sales ending. Follow the examples and acceptance rules in `CONTENT_PROCESS.md`.
